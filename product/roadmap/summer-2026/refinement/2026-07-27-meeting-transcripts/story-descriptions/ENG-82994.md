## Description (paste-ready)

Per ENG-82991. This story builds the **user-facing Zoom consent and connection UX**: the entry banner, the Integrations side panel where a user connects/disconnects Zoom, the confirmation + success feedback, and the connected-status indicators. Admin org-level OAuth is owned separately (ENG-82988 / ENG-83002) and is a precondition — there is **no per-user Zoom OAuth redirect**; the user's connect action is an in-app consent grant stored as a record in the Meetings service.

Consent controls whether a meeting's transcript is processed. A transcript-derived **Recap requires full consent from all participants**; if any participant has not consented, the transcript is not processed and both fall to **reduced mode** — prep and a meeting overview assembled from goals, open items, and notes, with no-transcript surfaces showing the **confidence-bar fallback**. Default posture is **opt-in with opt-out**, applied the same to Zoom and manual upload.

**Acceptance Criteria**

*Entry banner*
1. **Banner display** — *Given* the org has Zoom admin OAuth in place and the user has apps left to connect, *Then* a banner shows on the 1:1s list: "Connect your apps to make meetings more productive with AI-powered insights," with a **CONNECT** action and a dismiss (✕).
2. **Dismiss** — *When* the user dismisses the banner, *Then* it is hidden for that visit and reappears on the next visit until all relevant apps are connected.
3. **Open panel** — *When* the user taps CONNECT, *Then* the Integrations side panel opens.

*Integrations side panel*
4. **Panel structure** — *Given* the Integrations panel is open, *Then* it shows a **Calendar** category (Google Calendar, Outlook) and a **Meetings** category (Zoom); *And* the layout accommodates future integrations without redesign.
5. **Zoom card** — *Given* Zoom is not connected, *Then* the Zoom card shows its description ("Automatically generate AI-powered meeting prep, transcripts, action items, agenda topics, and recommendations from your Zoom meetings") and a **CONNECT** action.

*Connect (in-app consent, no OAuth redirect)*
6. **Confirmation modal** — *When* the user taps CONNECT on the Zoom card, *Then* a consent modal explains what is accessed (transcripts of meetings the user participates in), retention, **future meetings only** (no retroactive processing), and how to withdraw; with an **Accept** action and Cancel. *(Entry/card action label = "Connect"; the consent-modal confirm button = "Accept". Copy pending legal + InfoSec — see Dependencies.)*
7. **No redirect** — *Given* the user confirms, *Then* consent is stored as a record in the Meetings service; *And* no external Zoom OAuth redirect occurs.
8. **Success feedback** — *When* consent is stored, *Then* a success toast confirms the connection, *And* the Zoom card shows a Connected state with a **Disconnect** option, *And* the entry banner is removed without a full page reload.
9. **Connected indicator** — *Given* Zoom is connected, *Then* eligible 1:1 meeting cards show a Zoom indicator icon.

*Consent gating (full-consent) & reduced mode*
10. **Full consent for recap** — *Given* all participants have consented, *Then* the transcript is processed and transcript-grounded surfaces render (Recap, AI suggested items, transcript-derived goal recommendations).
11. **Non-consenting participant** — *Given* any participant has not consented, *Then* the transcript is not processed for that meeting, *And* both participants see **reduced mode**: prep + meeting overview from goals, open items, and notes only, with no-transcript surfaces showing the confidence-bar fallback.
12. **Third-party join** — *Given* an unplanned third party joins the meeting, *Then* the recap is discarded for that instance.

*Disconnect / withdraw*
13. **Withdraw** — *Given* Zoom is connected, *When* the user selects Disconnect / Withdraw consent in the panel, *Then* the consent record is removed; *And* no new transcripts are processed; *And* existing AI surfaces from prior meetings remain visible; *And* the entry banner reappears.
14. **Re-consent** — *Given* the user previously withdrew, *When* they connect again, *Then* processing resumes for future meetings only (no backfill).

*Admin-state transitions*
15. **Admin disconnects org-wide** — *Given* the admin revokes org Zoom access, *Then* user consent records move to paused, no new transcripts are processed, existing surfaces remain, *And* if the admin re-enables, valid consent records are restored without re-consent.
16. **Admin not connected** — *Given* org Zoom admin OAuth is not in place, *Then* no Zoom connect entry point is shown; *And* the panel may show "Ask your admin to enable Zoom for your organisation."

*Calendar-not-connected variant*
17. **Connect Zoom without calendar** — *Given* transcripts are associated to meetings via the **calendar service only**, *When* the user connects Zoom without a calendar connected, *Then* the flow prompts the user to connect a calendar, since Zoom alone cannot associate transcripts to 1:1 meetings. *(Pending confirmation with Anuj — see Open Questions / team-os open-questions Q18.)*

*Loading*
18. **Consent-check loading** — *Given* the meeting page is checking consent state, *Then* consent-dependent AI surfaces show a skeleton, resolving to either the connected surfaces or reduced mode.
