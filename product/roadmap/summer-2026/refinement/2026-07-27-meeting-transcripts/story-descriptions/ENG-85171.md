## Description (paste-ready)

Per ENG-82991 (Functional & UI Changes for AI-Powered 1:1s). This story adds the org-level admin setting that enables the new AI 1:1 insights (Prep, Recap, and suggestions) and retires the legacy 1:1 meeting summary.

The setting lives in **Program Management → AI → 1:1 AI Summary**. Enabling it is the org precondition that must be true before any user-level consent or transcript processing can occur.

**Setting**

- **Name:** *Enable New AI Insights for your organization*
- **Sub-text / tooltip:** *New AI 1:1 Insights generates meeting prep, recommendations, and recaps from meeting data and transcripts from the last 4 meetings, replacing the traditional 1:1 summary.*
- **Type:** Toggle, with a confirmation modal (*Enable New AI Insights for 1:1s* → Confirm / Cancel)

[Figma: link to the Program Management AI settings frame]

**Acceptance Criteria**

1. **Setting placement** — *Given* an admin is in Program Management → AI → 1:1 AI Summary, *Then* a toggle labelled *Enable New AI Insights for your organization* is present with its sub-text.
2. **Confirmation on enable** — *Given* the setting is off, *When* the admin toggles it on, *Then* a confirmation modal appears (*Enable New AI Insights for 1:1s* → Confirm / Cancel), *And* the setting changes only when the admin selects Confirm.
3. **Insight generation on enable** — *Given* the setting is enabled, *When* an upcoming 1:1 trigger fires, *Then* AI insights (prep, recap, suggestions) begin generating from that meeting's data.
4. **Empty states visible** — *Given* the setting is enabled, *When* a user opens an eligible 1:1 with no insights generated yet, *Then* the prep card, recap card, and suggestions queue render in their initial empty states.
5. **Legacy summary retired** — *Given* the setting is enabled, *Then* the existing 1:1 meeting-summary setting is disabled in both Program Management and Sysadmin settings.
6. **Legacy panel removed** — *Given* the setting is enabled, *When* a user views the right-hand context panel on a 1:1, *Then* the old AI meeting summary no longer renders.
7. **Disable path** — *Given* the setting is enabled, *When* the admin toggles it off, *Then* the legacy 1:1 summary setting becomes available again, *And* new AI insight generation stops for future triggers.
