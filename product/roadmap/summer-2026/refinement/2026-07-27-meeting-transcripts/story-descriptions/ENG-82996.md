## Description (paste-ready)

Per ENG-82991. UI for the **AI Meeting Prep card** in the right rail, shown pre-meeting. This ticket renders the prep payload produced by the AI platform (ENG-82387) — **no generation logic here**. The card is **persona-specific**: separate manager and report cards, stored as separate records; neither participant ever sees the other's. PII is handled upstream in AI inference.

Schema (locked 6/25): **ARC Synthesis / Attention Flags / Goal Signals**. The **manager card** additionally shows a **lightweight coaching_brief** (P1); the report card does not. Full coaching cards are Phase 2.

The upcoming-meeting design also surfaces elements not in the locked schema — a narrative summary, an **Inferred Topics** section, thumbs-up/down quality signals, a **Copy** action, and an "Always verify AI-generated content for accuracy" disclaimer. Which of these are P1 is unresolved — see Open Questions.

**Acceptance Criteria**

1. **Availability** — *Given* the prep brief has been generated, *When* the user opens the meeting in Upcoming state, *Then* the prep card renders in the right rail; *And* a loading state shows while generation is in progress.
2. **Persona isolation** — *Given* a viewer, *Then* the manager sees only the manager card and the report sees only the report card, enforced at the data layer; *And* if the viewer's role cannot be determined, the card is not shown.
3. **Schema render** — *Given* a prep payload, *Then* the card renders ARC Synthesis, Attention Flags, and Goal Signals.
4. **Manager coaching_brief** — *Given* the viewer is the manager, *Then* the card additionally shows a lightweight coaching_brief section; *Given* the viewer is the report, *Then* no coaching section is shown.
5. **Persona-specific refresh** — *Given* the meeting is Upcoming, *When* the user taps Refresh, *Then* only the requesting user's card regenerates; *And* the other persona's card is unaffected.
6. **Read-only after start** — *Given* the meeting reaches its scheduled start time, *Then* the card becomes read-only and collapses to an archived section in Active and Completed states.
7. **Staleness nudge** — *Given* agenda or action items change after generation while the meeting is Upcoming, *Then* a dismissible inline nudge is shown: "Your agenda has changed — Refresh prep?".
8. **Limited history** — *Given* fewer than four prior meetings exist, *Then* the card still renders from available context with no suppression.
9. **Empty** — *Given* insufficient context to generate a useful card, *Then* a graceful empty state is shown ("Not enough context yet to generate prep. This will improve after your first meeting.") with no error treatment.
10. **Error** — *Given* generation failed, *Then* a small inline nudge is shown ("Couldn't generate meeting prep this time.") with no retry; the next cycle retries.
11. **Collapsible** — *Given* the prep card is shown, *When* the user toggles Show more / Show less, *Then* the card expands or collapses.

**Open Questions** *(need answers before implementation)*

- Does the P1 prep schema include an **Inferred Topics** section (design shows it: AE shadow opportunities, Q2 quota, handoff progress) — the locked 6/25 schema lists only ARC Synthesis / Attention Flags / Goal Signals, so this either extends the payload contract or is deferred?
- What are the **canonical section labels** — the design shows an unlabeled narrative plus ATTENTION / GOALS / COACHING BRIEF / INFERRED TOPICS, while the schema names ARC Synthesis / Attention Flags / Goal Signals — which names drive the payload↔UI mapping?
- Are **thumbs-up/down quality signals** in P1 for the prep card, and do they write to the AI platform from day one as on the Recap? (adds an instrumentation contract to this ticket)
- Is the **Copy** action in P1, and does it copy the full prep or per-section? (defines a new interaction + clipboard format)
- Is the **"Always verify AI-generated content for accuracy"** disclaimer a required, legal-approved footer across all AI surfaces? (shared copy + legal gate)
- Grounding window — design says "based on last few transcripts"; is it fixed at the last four meetings or variable? (affects limited-history behaviour and payload)
- Attention flags carry **probing questions** ("Is there a dependency blocking this?") — display-only or actionable? (determines whether taps need handlers)
- For the **report's** card, is only Coaching Brief omitted, or Inferred Topics too — which sections are manager-only vs shared? (persona-conditional rendering rules)
