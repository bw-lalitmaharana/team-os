Consent Model (Phase 1)

Three levels of consent (locked 2026-07-08):

  1. Org — an admin enables the Betterworks Zoom app once per org.
  2. User — global “Connect Apps” side panel; covers all future series.
  3. Series — a “Sync meeting transcripts” checkbox, default ON with per-series opt-out. (Series checkbox ships in P2, after core ingestion + AI insights.)

Default posture (2026-07-21): opt-in with user opt-out, applied identically to Zoom and to manual uploads. Zoom does not itself require authorization to share transcripts; because performance conversations are sensitive, Betterworks defaults to “yes” with an explicit opt-out.

Transcript-Derived Output Rules

  • Full participant consent required for any transcript-derived Recap; any non-consenter excludes the transcript.
  • Not consent-gated: meeting notes, agenda, action items (structured meeting data).
  • An unplanned third-party join discards the entire recap.
  • Retroactive consent = no backfill.
  • Revoked consent halts future generation but retains past artifacts (final wording pending InfoSec).

Sensitivity & Existing Series

  • Per-instance “sensitive” flag — stored, never sent to AI; if applied post-ingestion, recap/prep regenerate without that instance (AI feasibility pending). (P2.)
  • Existing series get a Connect banner + confirmation pop-up (not a full re-flow).

Consent & Data-Handling Design — AI-Powered 1:1 Meetings (Zoom Transcripts)

For: Brian Jacobs (Head of InfoSec) · From: Lalit Maharana (PM) · Date: 2026-07-27 · Status: DRAFT for review. Summer 2026 launch is gated on this review.

1. What the feature does

For a Betterworks 1:1, the product generates a pre-meeting Prep brief, ingests the Zoom transcript (or a manual upload/paste) after the meeting, and produces an AI Recap + Goal recommendations. No AI output reaches a performance record without explicit user confirmation.

2. Data scope (locked)

  • Participants: the two direct 1:1 participants only; no third-party or non-human (bot/room) data enters the pipeline.
  • Sources: Zoom (native user-consented OAuth) + manual upload/paste; no bot-based recording (Recall.ai rejected, ADR-D-001).
  • Inference: private LLM stack only (Betterworks LM Engine, direct endpoint); no external LLM API calls.

3. Consent model

Three levels — org (admin enables app once), user (Connect Apps side panel), series (“Sync meeting transcripts” checkbox, default ON, per-series opt-out; ships P2). Default posture: opt-in with user opt-out, same for Zoom and manual uploads.

4. Transcript-derived output rules

  • Full participant consent required for any transcript-derived Recap; a non-consenter excludes the transcript.
  • Notes / agenda / action items are not consent-gated.
  • Unplanned third-party join → the recap is discarded.
  • Retroactive consent → no backfill. Revoked consent → future generation halts; past artifacts retained (⚠️ pending your input).

5. Sensitivity & exclusion controls

Per-instance “sensitive” flag — stored, never sent to AI; post-ingestion flagging regenerates without that instance (P2). Per-meeting exclusion / data-governance controls (ENG-82989).

6. Storage & retention  ⚠️ OPEN — needs InfoSec input

All sources route through S3 first, then the LM Engine; raw transcript persisted in S3 with a TTL (value not finalized — 2–6 mo proposed). Evidence rows in Aurora. For review: appropriate TTL for performance-conversation transcripts; encryption at rest/in transit; access boundaries; deletion on disconnect/revoke.
